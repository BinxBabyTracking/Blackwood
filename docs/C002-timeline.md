# C002 A Short Trip: canonical timeline

Internal case-authoring data. Players never see this as a whole; they rebuild it from statements, interviews and evidence. Every line of dialogue, the police tapes, the case file, the documents and the closing report agree with it, and `npm run check:case -- C002` guards the parts that can be checked mechanically.

Drawn from a real 2015 California abduction. Names, jobs, places and dates are changed, the year is moved to 1988, and nothing from the victim's captivity beyond the bare facts (held, blindfolded, sedated, released) is used. The full design is in the project doc "C002 Case Design - A Short Trip".

Status: approved for building, Oct 26, 2026.

## Fixed points

- Calendar, 1988: Oct 6 Thursday, Oct 9 Sunday, Oct 10 Monday, Oct 11 Tuesday, Oct 12 Wednesday, Oct 13 Thursday, Nov 4 Friday, Nov 7 Monday.
- Distances: Mariposa Bay to Tamarack Lake about 2 hours 40 minutes at night; Tamarack Lake to San Bernardino about 1 hour; Tamarack Lake to Long Beach about 2 hours 30 minutes.
- Area codes in 1988: Mariposa Bay (Ventura County) 805, Los Angeles 213, the San Bernardino Mountains 714.
- The Pine Ridge Lumber Co. mill whistle sounds at noon, Monday to Friday only.
- The Los Angeles Daily Star is a morning paper, on sale at the Tamarack Lake store by 7 AM.
- The investigator is hired on Tuesday, October 11, at 11:00 AM. Everything before that is reconstructed.

## Closing report only

No dialogue, knowledge or document may reveal these.

- 6:20 AM Monday to 5:30 AM Wednesday: Laura held in the back bedroom of Cabin 14, blindfolded, fed, sedated at night. Laura's own account covers only what she heard and smelled.
- Why he let her go: the Courier had the story, the police were involved, and he decided no ransom could be collected safely.
- That Oak Terrace was his second attempt at the same plan, for money he still needed.

## Contradictions found in the draft, and how they were resolved

1. **Postmark and trace on the same day.** The research outline had the tape received and the calls traced the same day as the postmark. Now the letter is mailed Monday from San Bernardino (so the postmark does not point at the lake) and the calls come Tuesday from the lake.
2. **The headline paper.** The local Courier is not sold at the lake, so Laura reads the Los Angeles Daily Star, which the ledger shows he bought.
3. **An evening paper at 7:50 AM.** The paper was first called the Evening Star. Renamed the Daily Star, a morning paper.
4. **Laboratory timing.** The design had the glasses' lab note already done by the police on Oct 11. Under the lab rule (nothing from the lab at the start) the police never took the glasses; the investigator finds them and has Bennett send them (E23). The typewriter comparison is likewise a lab test the player requests.
