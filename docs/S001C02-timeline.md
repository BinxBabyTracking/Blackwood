## The truth in brief

Jonathan Ellery, 41, a senior procurement analyst at Eastbridge Systems, a defense contractor in Reston, ran up about $94,000 of debt by early 2017, most of it his mother's memory-care bills and a business loan he had guaranteed for a friend. In June 2017 Julian Rourke, Director of Corporate Intelligence at Meridian Advisory Group in Washington, signed him as a paid "independent advisor" and had a Maryland lender, Northgate Bridge Lending LLC, clear his debts with a $60,000 "forgivable" loan and pay him $2,500 a month. Northgate is run from the office of Garrett Lyons, Rourke's operations coordinator. In return Jonathan passed Eastbridge's confidential pricing to Meridian, which sold it on to a competitor, Corvane Systems.

On October 30, 2018 Rourke asked for more: the names, home addresses and "anything personal" of the government evaluators on a contract award. Jonathan refused. Eastbridge had already traced the leak, a federal procurement task force was talking to everyone with access, and Jonathan's lawyer, Irene Chen, told him to tell them everything, his own part included. He agreed to a voluntary interview on Monday, November 19 at 9:30 AM. Lyons had been following him since October 30 and Meridian still read the mailbox it had given him in 2017, so they knew.

Rourke sent Lyons to bring Jonathan and his copies in. Lyons bought a hotel maintenance engineer, Kevin Hurst, who owed money to an offshore sportsbook. On Sunday, November 18 Jonathan checked into Room 614 of the Ashcroft Hotel, near Thomas Circle, so as not to sleep at home. Hurst gave Lyons the room number, issued him a temporary vendor badge, and bypassed the alarm on the sixth-floor service door under a maintenance pretext. At about 10:32 PM Jonathan opened his door to a man he knew. Lyons forced him to give up his laptop, tied his hands when he refused to say where his copies were, and walked him out through the service door and down the service elevator, which Hurst held for them, to the loading dock, out of sight of every guest-facing camera. By 12:16 AM Jonathan was locked in an upstairs room of a farmhouse on Sawmill Hollow Road in Frederick County, Maryland, leased by a Lyons company.

He had seen it coming. That Sunday afternoon he had left a sealed envelope with Irene Chen holding a PIN-locked encrypted drive: his own copies, the tasking emails, and a recording of Rourke telling him "everyone has a file." He never told anyone the code.

On Monday morning Rourke received a typed card: "Contain the Ellery exposure. Preserve the client structure. Report only when resolved. A." He kept it in his office safe. Adrian Holt (C01) had been in jail since Thursday, November 15.

**Solution.** Jonathan Ellery was kidnapped from Room 614. Garrett Lyons coordinated it and carried it out; Kevin Hurst gave him the room, the badge, the alarm bypass and the service elevator; Julian Rourke directed it, to stop Jonathan disclosing Meridian's intelligence operation and to recover his copies. Jonathan was taken out through the sixth-floor service door, the service elevator and the loading dock, and held at a Frederick County farmhouse leased by Lyons's company. Meridian's Corporate Intelligence division collected confidential and compromising information to influence and pressure people. Jonathan was not innocent: he sold his employer's information, then tried to come clean. The case does not identify "A".

### Contradictions found and fixed

- **Bible over brief, names:** Price to Ellery, Shaw to Rourke, Mercer to Lyons, Hurst's first name Kevin, Chen's Irene, Grant's Celia, Wallace's Douglas, Monroe's Tessa, Ellis to Okoye, Collins's Nadia, Voss to Lockhart, Cross to Holt, Bellwether to Carroway (the ending text), "the Price exposure" to "the Ellery exposure."
- **E033 had no source in the brief.** Bible: Capital Courier only, Tue Nov 20, triggered by D10, footer `MAG-RM 22-0187-009`. D10's effects send it (it is not one of the eight reactive events).
- **RE08 vs E036's source.** The brief makes E036 appear when D15 is made; the bible says it comes from Rourke's office safe in the search after the rescue. Both hold: D15 needs E023, which opens only with Jonathan's code after the rescue, so RE08 is the federal search of Rourke's office and Agent Quinlan's call, with E036 delivered.
- **E023 decryption.** A hardware PIN-pad drive that erases after ten wrong codes: nobody guesses, nobody brute-forces. Recovered in Chapter 3 (E022), opened in Chapter 4 by a forensic imaging lab once Jonathan gives the code.
- **E031** is found at Lyons's office (Kestrel's own alarm-monitoring report for the farmhouse), so the rescue never needs the player inside the property first.
- **The hub is in Pittsburgh and the case in Washington.** Natalie lives in Pittsburgh and is Bennett's physical therapist; the investigator drives down with her on Monday. Travel takes no game time, as in C01. The E033 courier is signed for by Bennett at the office and, like lab reports, can be read on the phone.
- **"Police department contact" in Act 1** is not one of the brief's 12 locations. Det. Collins is met in the hotel lobby (Chapters 1 to 3), by phone throughout, and at the farmhouse (Chapter 4). No police station scene.
- **Act 1 needs access logs** (D03 is E006 + E007) but the security office is an Act 2 location. Wallace comes up to the lobby in Chapter 1 with the lock audit and the corridor camera, and moves to his office in Chapter 2.
- **Multi-item deductions.** The engine confirms a deduction from one strung pair; D07, D09 and D15 (three items) and D13, D14 (deductions plus evidence) are pairs with `requires`. Deductions are never board cards in a case.
- **Interviewable count.** The brief's eleven, plus Agent Quinlan (bible: joins at the rescue, named in the closing), plus Bennett.
- **Tessa's visitor vs the timeline.** A 7:50 PM visit means Lyons drove down early: he left the farmhouse at 5:58 PM (E031) and passed Urbana at 6:21 PM (E027). It also explains why he needed Hurst for the room number at 8:20 PM.
- **"Jonathan never left through the main entrances."** True of every guest-facing camera; DOCK-1 records but is not on the monitor wall's main page, and nobody looked at it on Monday.
- **Hurst's bypass end.** The bypass ends on its 45-minute timer at 11:04 PM, so Hurst never had to go back up to the sixth floor.
- **Jonathan's testimony vs "nobody states the solution before the closing."** Jonathan says what he saw and heard: Lyons at the door and in the van, a man in a hotel work shirt holding the elevator whose name he never knew, Lyons on the phone to "Julian." He cannot say who arranged the hotel side, who paid whom, or what Rourke said or ordered. The report still needs the evidence.
- **"A" and Adrian Holt.** E034 carries "per A.H." (Holt's Carroway approvals): a deliberate trap. Holt has been held without bail in the Carroway County Jail since Nov 15; E036 is dated Nov 19.
- **Brief's ending text** says "Bellwether": now "Carroway." It can't mention "A" conditionally (series notebook text is fixed), so it doesn't name the letter; the case notebook does, on RE08.
