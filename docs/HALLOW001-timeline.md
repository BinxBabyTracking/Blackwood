# HALLOW001 The Thirteenth Guest: canonical timeline and build contract

Written before any dialogue. Everything in `src/cases/HALLOW001/` must agree with this file. Source design: the project doc "HALLOW001 Case Design - The Thirteenth Guest" (v1.1). This file supersedes it where they differ; the differences are listed under "Changes from the design".

Seasonal: October only (`available_month` 10). 1 to 6 investigators. Tier 2, about 2 to 3 hours.

- **Evelyn's age.** Josie is 29: she was 9 in 1998.
- **Evidence ids** are HE001 to HE036, deductions HD01 to HD13. Two more items exist only if the killer acts: HE037 (the report of a second attack) and HE038 (burned papers in the greenhouse stove).
- **The study is upstairs.** The design put the study on the main floor but has the scream, and the thirteenth guest, in the upstairs corridor. The study is at the end of the upstairs corridor. The servants' stair runs from behind its shelves down to the old service corridor in the lower level, ending in the short flight of stone steps where Annabel fell in 1998.
- **Blackwood's base** is the Morning Room on the main floor, given to the agency by the police as an incident room (case file, board, phone, map). Every investigator carries a phone (`phone: "mobile"`).
- **The clock** runs on what the investigators do (travel 3 minutes, examining something new 1, each question or presentation 1, each call 1), starting at 10:32 PM. Real time plays no part.
- **Escape and the midnight procession.** Crane can only leave by the front gate (his car is in the gatehouse lot) or by the back lane past the maze, and only by the lane if he has called his driver. Investigators can overhear that call.
- **Ending E (the wrong accusation)** is not a terminal ending: the engine returns a wrong report, as in every case. A returned report emboldens Crane (he may take the envelope from the library globe before it is found) and costs leaderboard points.

## Setting

Blackthorn Estate, Hollins Road, Harrow Falls, Berkshire County, Massachusetts. A Victorian stone house of 1903 with a carriage house, gatehouse, greenhouse and yew maze. Home of Ambrose Blackthorn, antiquarian, chairman of the Blackthorn Foundation (historic preservation grants), which keeps its archive in the house library. Desmond Crane is the foundation's treasurer and trustee since 1994 and was Ambrose's partner in Blackthorn & Crane Antiquities until 2009.

Halloween 2018 is a Wednesday. Rain all night, fog on the lawns. Halloween 1998 was a Saturday.

### Crane's account (his knowledge, lies included)

He left at 10:08 to take a call from his Boston office in the conservatory, where the signal is best; he was there in the dark when the lights went and came through when he heard the scream. He has never been in the servants' stair, which "hasn't been opened since the nineties." He did not know about any thirteenth guest. He suggests the veiled woman is an intruder. Nobody's knowledge confesses.

### Closing report only

Not said by anyone, anywhere: the shove at 8:51 PM in 1998; Crane waiting by the panel at 10:10; the blow; what Crane said in the study; the folder going into the port bins.

## The killer (rule-driven)

Crane's place is env `crane` (dining room at the start). He notices only what he could see or hear from the main floor: an officer posted at the gate, the commotion when the study shelves are opened, an investigator sitting with Harriet over her ledger, Bridget talking about 1998, the veiled woman brought downstairs, police lights at the carriage house, a report that comes back wrong. He never knows what is in the notebook.

| Move | When | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spread the intruder story | 10:45 PM on, the veiled woman not found | Felix and Constance repeat it |
| Call his driver | 11:00 PM on | He goes to the conservatory for a few minutes and calls; investigators there can overhear |
| Leave a note for Bridget | He fears Bridget | HE036 exists |
| Recover the folder | He knows the stair is found, the folder not yet found | He takes it from the cellar |
| Burn the folder | After recovering it | HE038 in the greenhouse stove |
| Take the globe envelope | A report came back wrong, the envelope not yet found | HE035 lost |
| Attack a witness | 11:20 PM on, he is cornered (below), he fears that witness, nobody from Blackwood is in the room with them, nobody has been attacked | Unguarded: killed (Ending C). Quietly guarded: interrupted and arrested (Ending B). One attack at most; there is never a third death. |
| Escape by the gate | 11:40 PM on, he is cornered, no officer at the gate | Gone (Ending D) |
| Escape by the lane | He is cornered, the gate is watched, he has called his driver; 11:40 PM on, or during the procession | Officer on the lane: caught and arrested. Otherwise gone. |

**Cornered** means the police have opened the carriage house chamber, or he knows the stair has been found and fears at least one witness. Each open move has its own chance each tick (an action that moves the clock), rolled from the investigation's seed.

**What he notices**, and only from the main floor: the commotion when the study shelves are opened (half the time, each tick); the crime scene unit at the carriage house; an investigator with Harriet over her ledger, seen from the dining room or library (half the time); Amos telling an investigator about the stair; Bridget talking about 1998 in the dining room (half the time); the veiled woman brought downstairs; a returned report. When he starts to fear a witness, an investigator in the same room sees him watching them.

Two officers: each can be posted once (the gate, visibly; the back lane, quietly; or quietly with one witness). If neither is posted by 11:00 PM, Lt. Mahoney calls to ask. She detains Crane when asked, once the investigators can show motive and opportunity (HD09 and HD10).

## Contradictions found while writing

1. The design had the study on the main floor and the scream upstairs. The study is upstairs (see Changes).
2. The design had "no one visible on the security cameras" but the veiled woman arriving. The gate camera shows her arrive at 8:40 PM and nobody leave: she is still in the house.
3. A blackout would stop the cameras. The gatehouse and its cameras run on their own supply.
4. Crane could not come back up the kitchen stair without meeting Amos at 10:18. He goes out by the cellar's outside door and comes in through the conservatory, which is how Penrose and Constance see him.
