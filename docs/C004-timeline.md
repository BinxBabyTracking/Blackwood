# C004 Canonical Timeline: The Last Broadcast

Chicago, October 1927. A Historical Case File: Blackwood holds the 1927 file of the Ashby and Lowe Detective Bureau, and the player is that bureau's investigator. Every document, answer and event in `src/cases/C004/` must agree with this table.

Calendar checked: Oct 18, 1927 is a Tuesday; Oct 19 Wednesday; Oct 20 Thursday; Oct 21 Friday; Oct 22 Saturday; Oct 24 Monday; Oct 25 Tuesday.

Artistic license, agreed Oct 27, 2026: the station's disc recorder cuts the 7:10 PM rehearsal on aluminum and it can be played back that night. The case never draws attention to it.

## Closing report only

No dialogue or knowledge may reveal these.

- What was said in Studio A at 9:50, and how the blow was struck, told plainly.
- That Harlan's plan at 9:05 was to keep Arthur off the air, not to kill him.
- What Harlan meant to do with the investigator at the warehouse. Nothing in play shows harm.

## Contradictions found

- The spec's 9:05 schedule change came before an unplanned killing. Fixed: Harlan meant to silence Arthur, not kill him; the disc he had arranged became his alibi.
- The engineer would see into the studio. Fixed: Harlan drew the curtain at 9:10.
- A 1927 doctor cannot tell 9:55 from 10:15. Fixed: the coroner widens the window (9:30 to 10:40); the disc, the register and Rose fix the rest.
- Samuel "unknowingly" helped: he knew it was a disc, not that Arthur was dead. He lied to save his license.
- Henry has two addresses: his apartment was padlocked on Oct 15; he rooms at Doyle's.
- Harlan "heard Arthur live at 10:15"; the line register shows PHONO 2. Deliberate.
- Harlan "in his office from 9:30"; his own book says Studio A at 9:50 and Clara puts him in storage at 10:02. Deliberate.
- Russo "never met Harlan"; his note says "V.H. squares the books." Deliberate.
