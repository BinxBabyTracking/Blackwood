# C003 Timeline: The Witness on Alder Street

The canonical timeline for C003. Every file in `src/cases/C003/` agrees with it; where a document or answer gives a time, it is the time here. Copied from the design doc (project doc "C003 Case Design - The Witness on Alder Street", Draft 2), which also holds every evidence text in full.

Fixed references: Calder County (fictional); Bellwether PD case 18-09-1147; the crash outside 412 Alder Street, the Marshes at 409; Hart's Quick Stop, 1820 Furnace Street; Bell Security, 1150 Mill Road; Shaw Towing, 4410 Route 51; Riverside Commons, 2200 River Road. Phones: Bell (412) 555-0187, Shaw (724) 555-0163, the prepaid (724) 555-0149, Vivian (724) 555-0122.

Change from the design doc made while building: Det. Cross and Dr. Reed have no question limit (like Bennett), because the call that brings police to the Marsh house and every lab test go through them, so running out of questions can never make the case unsolvable.

No dialogue or knowledge may reveal these.

- The September 13 meeting at the site trailer, and what Voss told them: get the bag, scare him, nobody gets hurt.

### Contradictions found

- The spec placed Vivian's realization on Sept 28 with no reason. Fixed: the Friday night football game that evening, where Bell Security did parking.
- The spec had Ethan discovering on Sept 10 and copying on Sept 12 with no annex visit between. Fixed: he views the original on Sept 11 (access log) and copies on Sept 12.
- A trim fragment on a street for six weeks needs a reason: it is wedged in the hedge roots under the tree, where the police search missed it.
- Bell says Unit 4 was stolen "overnight" from the garage; the GPS shows it leaving at 6:52 PM. Deliberate, for the player.
- Bell says the deer strike was Sept 15; the fragment was at the scene on Sept 14. Deliberate.
- Shaw's dispatch log has his "Route 51" call, but his truck's GPS sits at the Bell garage 9:15 to 10:38 PM, and his phone used a tower near Alder Street at 10:05 PM. Deliberate.
- The second-crime evidence must not exist as collectible evidence before the midpoint: E17, E18, E19, E24 are inside the Marsh house and only become hotspots after MID2.

## The midpoint

Four story steps, separate from the six reactive events. Each fires once per game, server side, and is shared by everyone in a joint game.

| Step | Fires when | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MID1 Vivian's call | D01, D02 and D08 made; Vivian interviewed at least once; Bell interviewed at least once | Her call rings on the player's phone wherever they are (after any interview in progress). "I've been thinking about what you asked me. About the vehicle. I remembered something, and I think you need to hear it. I'd rather not say it over the phone. Could you come by tonight, around eight?" From now on Vivian and Graham cannot be interviewed. A notebook objective: Meet Vivian Marsh, 8 PM |
| MID2 The Marsh house | The player next travels to L06 | Notice: "Monday, October 29, 8:10 PM." L06 shows its after state. New hotspots: the door (E19), the kitchen calendar (E17), Vivian's notebook on the table (E18), the open laptop (E24). Objective: Call Det. Cross |
| MID3 Police notified | The player calls Cross (any words about the Marshes) | Cross: officers on the way, don't touch anything else. Police overlay on L06. Reed offers to test the smear (lab test for E20) |
| MID4 The bodies | MID3 done and the player leaves L06 | Notice: "Tuesday, October 30, 7:40 AM." Cross calls: the Marshes were found in a burned SUV on the riverside access road; it is registered to Bell Security, which reported it stolen this morning. Case status becomes ACTIVE MULTIPLE HOMICIDE. Vivian and Graham are listed as victims, interviews marked deceased (past conversations stay in the notebook). L11 unlocks. The board's second section opens. New demeanors and re-interview grants for Rachel, Cross, Bell, Shaw, Voss |

The murders happen before the player can arrive; there is no timer, no countdown and no penalty for delay. If the player goes elsewhere after MID1, the visit waits. Nothing anyone says suggests the player caused it. Rachel, Cross and Bennett say plainly that it was not the investigator's doing.
