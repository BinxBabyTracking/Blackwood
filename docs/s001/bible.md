## 4. The leak channel (the Judas thread)

This is the spine the briefs were missing. Evelyn sacrifices her operators with true documents, routed so they look anonymous.

**How it works.** Castell relays Evelyn's instructions to Claire as "what the investigators need next." Claire pulls the named record from the Meridian archive, copies it on the archive copier, and hands the copy to Castell. Clearwater ships it by Capital Courier, account 20417. The archive copier prints a footer on every copy: `COPY · MAG-RM` plus a collection, box and item number. Collection 14 is finance, 22 is client services, 31 is logistics. Nobody can read the code until C04-E015 (the Document Classification Guide) explains it.

## 5. The rolling board and series system

Built once, in the S01 C01 chat.

**Series progress.** One record per player per series: `seriesId`, cases unlocked and completed, the rolling board (pins, positions, notes, labels, string), series deductions made, last played. Standalone cases never touch it; S002 gets its own.

**One board.** There is exactly one rolling board per series save. Pin or remove anything, from any case, and every case sees the change, because there is only the one board. That is how "go back to an old case, add evidence, and later cases update" works.

**What can be pinned.** Any evidence the player has discovered in any S001 case, any photo, and any series deduction already made, referenced by canonical ID (`S001-C01-E011`). Nothing is copied. Evidence can sit on both boards at once.

**When it appears.** After S01 C01's report is accepted: the closing office sequence wheels it in. From then on it is in the office during C02 to C06, and also when the player opens a closed case's file from the archive.

**Never automatic.** No auto-pins, no auto-string, no auto-deductions, no auto-layout.

**Series deductions.** As C003 and C004 do on the case board: the player strings a pair, and the deduction completes if every other required item or deduction is also pinned on the rolling board. Made deductions appear as cards that can be pinned. A deduction once made stays made, even if its cards are later unpinned.

**Replays.** "A replay starts a clean file" applies to the case only. Replaying C01 never touches the rolling board; anything newly found in the replay becomes pinnable.

**Joint play.** The table plays the host's save, so it uses the host's series board. The wheel-in sequence plays once per save. Guests see the host's board.

**Store and page.** Cases sell one at a time or as a bundle; each opens only after the previous one is solved, whichever way it was bought. The series page has the series title image with the six case squares in a grid under it, labeled S01 C01 to S01 C06. Future case titles are shown; their descriptions and solutions are not.

**Finale ending.** The closing camera moves across the player's actual rolling board, never a prearranged version. That needs a board renderer the ending can drive.
