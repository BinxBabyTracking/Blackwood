## Opening premise

Three days before the end of 2018, Blackwood receives a message from an unidentified former Meridian employee.

The message contains a short excerpt from an internal authorization register.

It references the financial operations exposed in Bellwether, the intelligence operation uncovered in Washington, and the criminal freight network investigated in Baltimore.

The sender claims to possess the original records identifying the person who authorized these operations.

The message ends:

"If you want to prove who built it, find the people who kept the records."

Before Blackwood can arrange a meeting, the sender disappears.

At the same time:

- A Meridian records facility reports a suspicious fire.
- Several company accounts begin closing.
- Former associates receive threatening communications.
- Claire Donovan reports that someone has tried to contact her.
- Evelyn Voss announces a restructuring of the Voss Foundation's investment holdings.

Blackwood must find the missing records administrator before the organization destroys its remaining evidence.

# 2. CANONICAL SERIES TRUTH

AUTHOR-ONLY INFORMATION.

Do not expose this section in player-visible case data or dialogue until supported by discovered evidence.

## The mastermind

Dr. Evelyn Voss, age 52, is the Architect.

She established Meridian Advisory Group in approximately 2007 as a legitimate consulting business that also provided the operational structure for a criminal network.

Evelyn's philosophy is based on control through information and dependency.

She believes powerful people can be directed by discovering what they fear losing.

She uses:

- Financial dependence
- Compromising information
- Corporate relationships
- Charitable influence
- Political access
- Intermediaries
- Selective disclosure
- Intimidation
- Carefully controlled criminal operations

She does not personally commit most of the network's crimes.

Instead, she directs trusted operators who manage separate functions.

## The four principal operators

Adrian Cross:
Financial transfers, shell companies, fraudulent contracts.

Marcus Shaw:
Intelligence gathering, surveillance, blackmail, coercion.

Victor Kane:
Criminal logistics, intimidation, illicit shipments.

Elena Ward:
Legal concealment, ownership structures, political influence, suppression of damaging information.

These individuals possessed different levels of knowledge.

They were not necessarily aware of every operation.

Evelyn maintained compartmentalization to prevent any one subordinate from exposing the entire network.

## The betrayal

During 2018, Adrian, Marcus, and Victor became increasingly reckless.

Their activities created financial exposure, unnecessary violence, and risks to the organization.

Evelyn decided to sacrifice them.

She encouraged Claire Donovan to release genuine records that would expose those operators.

She carefully selected information that implicated them while concealing her own command role.

Blackwood followed those leads and solved real crimes.

Evelyn benefited from the investigations.

This is the central meaning of The Judas Principle.

The betrayal was carried out through truth rather than fabricated evidence.

## The final mistake

Evelyn believed the original command records had been destroyed or rendered inaccessible.

However, a longtime Meridian records administrator preserved a separate archive.

That archive contains original authorization records, communications, and corroborating financial documentation.

The records can establish Evelyn's personal involvement in directing criminal operations.

Evelyn learns that the archive may still exist.

She initiates an effort to recover or destroy it.

That effort creates the final investigation.

# 3. THE MISSING WITNESS

## CH01: Benjamin Vale

Age: 58
Occupation: Former Meridian records administrator
Residence: Arlington, Virginia
Status: Missing at opening, alive at conclusion

Benjamin worked for Meridian for approximately ten years.

He managed executive records, retention schedules, document authentication, and secure archival systems.

He was not a senior criminal operator.

He knew that some records concerned illegal activity but remained employed because he feared retaliation and believed he could preserve evidence.

He secretly retained authenticated copies of important executive records.

## What Benjamin possesses

Benjamin holds an encrypted archive containing:

- Original executive authorizations
- Financial approval records
- Communications between Evelyn and principal operators
- Records of selective disclosure instructions
- Legal concealment directives
- Instructions concerning specific criminal operations
- Internal references connecting "A" and "The Architect" to Evelyn
- Evidence of attempted record destruction
- Metadata and custody records needed for authentication

## Benjamin's disappearance

Benjamin contacted Blackwood after learning that Meridian was destroying records.

Before meeting the investigators, he noticed surveillance near his residence.

He fled voluntarily.

He left a sequence of clues designed to help a legitimate investigator locate him.

He did not initially trust law enforcement because he feared the organization had compromised officials.

However, Evelyn's contractors independently began searching for him.

Benjamin eventually becomes trapped at a temporary hiding location.

He must be recovered before the archive can be authenticated.

## Important

Benjamin is not murdered.

The player must recover him alive.

There must be no unavoidable timed failure.

His disappearance should initially appear ambiguous.

The player must distinguish voluntary flight from subsequent coercive pursuit.

The archive is the central evidentiary objective.

It must not function as a magical document that instantly proves every crime.

Players must recover, unlock, examine, authenticate, and corroborate its contents.

## Archive components

### A. Authorization Register

Records executive approval codes and associated operations.

### B. Executive Correspondence

Contains communications linking Evelyn to criminal directives.

### C. Financial Control Records

Shows Evelyn's authority over intermediary companies and operational budgets.

### D. Intelligence Directives

Connects Evelyn to selected surveillance and coercion programs.

### E. Enforcement Records

Shows her authorization of intimidation and concealment operations.

### F. Disclosure Selection Records

Shows Evelyn deliberately chose which records Claire would release to Blackwood.

### G. Records Destruction Instructions

Shows Evelyn directed the destruction or concealment of evidence after the previous investigations.

### H. Authentication Records

Contains provenance, signatures, contemporaneous metadata, access records, and other information needed to assess authenticity.

## Required distinction

The archive proves Evelyn directed criminal operations and knowingly authorized certain unlawful acts.

It does not automatically prove that she specifically ordered every murder committed by her subordinates.

Preserve the canonical distinctions:

Paul Reddick murdered Martin Keller.

Thomas Bell murdered Samuel Ortiz.

Gabriel Knox murdered Nathaniel Brooks.

Those individuals remain directly responsible for their actions.

Adrian, Victor, and Elena directed associated theft, intimidation, or concealment operations according to the evidence established in earlier cases.

Evelyn's responsibility must be grounded in authenticated instructions, knowledge, and acts attributable to her.

Do not turn her into a mastermind who secretly ordered every killing merely for dramatic effect.
